About

A Decade In
High Performance

Adam is a strength and conditioning coach with almost a decade of experience in high-performance sport. He specialises in the physical qualities that matter most in field and court-based sports — speed, acceleration, strength, and change-of-direction ability.

His Master's degree in Strength & Conditioning underpins a practical, evidence-based approach to coaching. Sport science isn't treated as theory separate from practice — it informs training design, athlete monitoring, and day-to-day decisions on the floor.

A significant part of his work takes place within the BSC Young Boys Academy, where he works with players in the final stage of development before professional football. Beyond football, he works across a range of performance environments — including Ballet Company Bern, floorball, and Muay Thai — where the physical demands differ but the same principles of structured, targeted training apply.

Clear communication and the coach–athlete relationship sit at the heart of his practice. In many environments, the S&C coach is the first and last point of contact in an athlete's day. He works closely with athletes to understand their situation, navigate setbacks, and maintain steady progress — because effective coaching is inseparable from the relationships built around it.
