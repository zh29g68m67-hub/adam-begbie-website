Team & Club Consultancy

Sport science expertise integrated into your programme without the overhead of a full-time hire. Working alongside existing staff to elevate your squad's physical preparation strategy.

  • Physical preparation strategy audit and design
  • Periodisation planning aligned with competition schedule
  • Staff upskilling and CPD sessions
  • Injury prevention screening and protocol design
  • Match-week and pre-season structure development

04

Sport Science Testing

Stop guessing — start measuring. A structured battery of strength, speed, and movement tests to establish your athletic baseline, identify priorities, and track adaptation over time.

  • Force-velocity profiling (bilateral & unilateral)
  • Jump testing: CMJ, SJ, drop jump (VALD ForceDecks)
  • Sprint testing: 10m, 30m, flying 10m
  • Strength & power benchmarks
  • Detailed written report with programming recommendations

How It Works

The Process

01

Initial Consultation

A 30-minute call to understand your goals, current training, competition schedule, and any constraints or injury history.

02

Assessment

Movement screening, strength testing, and physical profiling to establish a data-informed baseline and identify priority areas.

03

Programme Design

A fully periodised programme built around your profile — structured in phases with clear progressions, targets, and testing points.

04

Ongoing Delivery

Regular sessions, monitoring check-ins, and programme adjustments to keep adaptation on track as your season and circumstances evolve.
