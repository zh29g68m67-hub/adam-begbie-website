Questions

FAQ

All programmes are designed for a standard commercial gym with barbells, dumbbells, a squat rack, and cable machines. The Foundation programme has a minimal-equipment variant for home or limited-equipment environments. If you're unsure, get in touch before purchasing.

All programmes are delivered via TrainHeroic, which is free to download on iOS and Android. You'll receive a login link after purchase. The app lets you log each session, track loads over time, and access video exercise demonstrations.

The Foundation and Performance Block programmes can be adapted for in-season use by reducing session volume and adjusting intensity around competition. The Competition Peak programme is specifically designed for the final 8 weeks before a major event. If you need a fully customised in-season programme, consider 1:1 or remote coaching instead.

The Foundation and Performance Block programmes are self-guided — the app provides all the guidance you need. The Competition Peak programme includes an email Q&A option (48-hour response). If you want regular direct coaching support, the Remote Programming service includes weekly check-ins and video feedback.

If you're unsure which programme suits you, get in touch before purchasing — I'd rather you buy the right thing than the wrong one. Each programme page includes a suitability guide. If you have specific injury history, constraints, or goals that don't fit any of the programmes, remote coaching may be a better fit.
